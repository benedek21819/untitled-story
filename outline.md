# Outline

## Premise

_One or two sentences: who wants what, and what stands in the way._

## Themes

_What the story is actually about, underneath the plot._

## Structure

### Act I — Setup

- [ ] Opening image / status quo
- [ ] Inciting incident
- [ ] The decision that locks the protagonist in

### Act II — Confrontation

- [ ] First real cost
- [ ] Midpoint reversal
- [ ] Everything falls apart

### Act III — Resolution

- [ ] The choice that proves the change
- [ ] Climax
- [ ] Closing image

## Open Questions

_Things not decided yet. Move them up into the structure as they resolve._
